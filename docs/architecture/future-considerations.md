# Future Considerations

## FC-001 - Fictional Enterprise Policy

Status: Future Consideration

Date: 2026-07-19

Priority: Medium

Motivation

As enterprise generation becomes increasingly realistic, automatically generated organizations may unintentionally resemble existing companies.

Potential Concerns

- Legal considerations
- User expectations
- Brand confusion
- Training authenticity

Possible Solutions

- Similarity validation
- Fictional-by-default policy
- User disclaimer
- Enterprise Twin module

Review Trigger

Revisit after the Enterprise Creation Engine reaches feature completeness.

## FC-002 - AI-Driven Stakeholder Simulation

Status: Future Consideration

Date: 2026-07-25

Priority: Medium

### Motivation

As the enterprise simulation environment matures, introduce AI-driven stakeholder interactions to allow users to practice discovery, assessment, and implementation activities in a realistic organizational context.

The objective is not to replace implementation logic, but to simulate the human and organizational aspects involved in IAM projects.

### Concept

The AI would represent different stakeholders within a fictional organization, allowing users to conduct discovery discussions and gather information required to understand the environment.

Possible simulated stakeholders:

- Executive Sponsor / Project Owner
- Human Resources
- IT Management
- Identity Administrators
- Information Security
- Application Owners
- Service Desk
- Business Representatives

### Potential Capabilities

- Stakeholder-specific conversations based on role and knowledge level
- Different perspectives between departments
- Hidden organizational issues discovered through investigation
- Realistic business constraints
- Conflicting information requiring validation
- Resistance to proposed changes
- Evolution of company maturity over time

### Possible Use Cases

- IAM discovery practice
- Stakeholder interview training
- Assessment exercises
- Solution design scenarios
- Security maturity simulations

### Dependencies

- Enterprise generation engine
- Scenario definition framework
- IAM methodology documentation
- Simulation event system
- AI integration layer

### Risks / Considerations

- Maintaining realistic but fictional organizations
- Preventing overly predictable responses
- Ensuring technical accuracy
- Avoiding replacing human decision-making with automated recommendations

### Review Trigger

Revisit after the core enterprise simulation and scenario engine reach sufficient maturity.
