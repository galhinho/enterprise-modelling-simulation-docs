# Enterprise

Status: 📝 Draft

## Purpose

This document defines the concept of an Enterprise within the Enterprise Modelling & Simulation.

The Enterprise represents the primary entity being modeled by the simulator.

An Enterprise is not a Domain itself.

Instead, an Enterprise is a coordinated system composed of multiple interconnected Domains that work together to create, deliver, preserve, support, or otherwise contribute value.

---

## Definition

An Enterprise is an entity created to pursue one or more value objectives through coordinated effort.

To achieve these objectives, An Enterprise combines Business,
Business Capabilities, Organization, People, Identity, Knowledge, and Technology into a structured and interdependent system.

An Enterprise may exist in many forms, including:

- Commercial Organizations
- Government Agencies
- Non-Profit Organizations
- Educational Institutions
- Research Organizations
- Healthcare Providers
- Community Organizations

These examples are not exhaustive.

---

## Enterprise Components

An Enterprise is composed of multiple interconnected Domains.

### Core Domains

Core Domains define the primary structure of the Enterprise.

- Business
- Organization

### Supporting Domains

Supporting Domains enable and support Enterprise participation and operation.

- People
- Identity
- Knowledge
- Technology

### Foundational Concepts

Foundational concepts provide direction and purpose for the Enterprise.

- Value
- Business Capabilities

---

### Foundational Principle

An Enterprise exists to create, deliver, preserve, support, or otherwise contribute one or more forms of value.

The structure and components of an Enterprise should ultimately support that objective.

---

### Enterprise Formation

Enterprises are typically created in response to a perceived need, opportunity, or value gap.

The purpose of the Enterprise is to create, deliver, preserve, support, or otherwise contribute value intended to address that need.

The Enterprise achieves this through the coordinated use of Business, Business Capabilities, Organization, People, Identity, Knowledge, and Technology.

---

## Relationship to Value

Value provides purpose and direction to the Enterprise.

Value answers:

Why should the Enterprise exist?

The Enterprise answers:

How can value be systematically pursued, delivered, preserved, supported, or created?

Value therefore serves as the foundational driver of Enterprise creation.

---

## Design Principles

### An Enterprise Is Not a Domain

An Enterprise is not a standalone Domain.

Instead, an Enterprise represents the coordinated combination of multiple Domains working together toward one or more value objectives.

---

### An Enterprise Is a System

An Enterprise should be viewed as an interconnected system rather than a collection of independent components.

Changes within one Domain may influence multiple other Domains.

---

### Domains Exist in Service of the Enterprise

Business,
Business Capabilities,
Organization,
People,
Identity,
Knowledge,
and Technology
exist to support the Enterprise's ability to pursue value objectives.

---

## Architectural Observation

An Enterprise should be viewed as the highest-level static construct within Enterprise Creation.

The Enterprise provides the context within which all Domains, Business Capabilities, and Value Objectives are organized.

Future operational and simulation concepts should build upon the Enterprise rather than redefine it.
