# Value

Status: 📝 Draft

---

## Purpose

This document defines the concept of Value within the Enterprise Modelling & Simulation.

Value represents the fundamental reason for enterprise existence.

Every enterprise exists to create, deliver, preserve, support, or otherwise contribute some form of value.

Value acts as a foundational influence during Enterprise Creation.

Value guides the definition of Business, Business Capabilities, Organizational Structures, People, Identities, Knowledge, and Technology by providing purpose and direction for the Enterprise.

---

## Definition

Value represents the intended benefit, outcome, or contribution that justifies the existence of an enterprise.

Without value, an enterprise lacks purpose.

Value may be directed toward:

- Customers
- Members
- Stakeholders
- Communities
- Governments
- Society
- The Enterprise Itself

---

## Examples

Examples of value may include:

- Profit
- Healthcare
- Education
- Research
- Security
- Public Service
- Entertainment
- Transportation
- Manufacturing
- Innovation
- Social Impact

These examples are not exhaustive.

---

## Relationship to Enterprise

Value serves as the foundation of enterprise creation.

Value influences:

- Business Objectives
- Business Capabilities
- Organizational Structure
- People Requirements
- Identity Requirements
- Knowledge Requirements
- Technology Requirements

As a result, Value may be considered the starting point that influences and guides Enterprise Creation.

---

## Relationship to Enterprise Creation

Value is established during Enterprise Creation.

Enterprise Creation begins by identifying the value the enterprise intends to create, deliver, preserve, or support.

This value provides direction for the subsequent definition of Business, Business Capabilities, Organization, People, Identity, Knowledge, and Technology.

---

## Relationship to Business

Business defines how value is pursued.

Value answers:

Why should the enterprise exist?

Business answers:

How will the enterprise pursue and deliver value?

---

## Design Principles

### Value Precedes Business

An enterprise should first establish the value it seeks to create before defining business objectives and capabilities.

---

### Value Influences Enterprise Design

Enterprise structures should ultimately support the creation, delivery, preservation, or support of value.

---

### Value Is Foundational

Value is not a capability, technology, organization, person, identity, or knowledge construct.

Instead, Value provides purpose and direction to those constructs.

---

### Value Is Not Limited To Financial Return

Value represents the benefit created for one or more beneficiaries.

Financial return is one possible outcome of value creation, but it is not a prerequisite for value to exist.

This allows the simulator to represent commercial enterprises, public institutions, healthcare providers, educational organizations, non-profit organizations, research institutions, and community initiatives using the same foundational concept.

---

## Architectural Observation

Value may be viewed through two perspectives.

### Static Value

Represents the initial purpose and intended value of the enterprise during creation.

The current model focuses on Static Value.

### Dynamic Value

Represents the operational state of enterprise value.

While Static Value defines the initial purpose of the enterprise, Dynamic Value represents how value priorities may evolve during enterprise operation.

Dynamic Value is outside the scope of Enterprise Creation.

How Value evolves, changes priority, competes with other objectives, or responds to operational conditions belongs to Enterprise Operation and Simulation.

---

## Beneficiaries

Every Value is intended to benefit one or more beneficiaries.

Beneficiaries may include:

- Customers
- Employees
- Patients
- Students
- Citizens
- Communities
- Partners
- Governments
- Society
- The Enterprise Itself

Beneficiaries are not limited to the Enterprise itself.

---

### Value Measurement

Value may be evaluated through measurable outcomes aligned with its intended purpose.

Measures will vary depending on the nature of the Enterprise and the Value being pursued.

Detailed Value measurement is considered future work.
