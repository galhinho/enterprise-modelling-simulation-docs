# Domain Classification

Status: 📝 Draft

---

# Purpose

This document defines how Domains are classified within the Enterprise Modelling & Simulation.

Domain classification does not represent importance, complexity, or business value.

Instead, classification reflects the role a Domain plays in defining or enabling an Enterprise.

---

## Importance vs Classification

Domain classification does not indicate importance.

A Supporting Domain may be critical to Enterprise success.

For example:

- Technology is classified as a Supporting Domain because it enables capabilities rather than defining enterprise purpose or structure.
- Technology remains essential to the operation of most modern enterprises.

Classification reflects architectural responsibility rather than business importance.

---

# Core Domains

Core Domains define the fundamental purpose, structure, and composition of an Enterprise.

Current Core Domains include:

- Business
- Organization

Core Domains answer questions such as:

- Why does the Enterprise exist?
- What must the Enterprise accomplish?
- How is the Enterprise structured?

---

# Supporting Domains

Supporting Domains enable, support, represent, or facilitate Enterprise participation and operation.

Current Supporting Domains include:

- People
- Identity
- Knowledge
- Technology

Supporting Domains answer questions such as:

- Who participates?
- How is participation represented?
- What is known?
- How are capabilities enabled?

---

# Important Observation

Supporting Domains are not less important than Core Domains.

A Supporting Domain may be critical to Enterprise success.

Classification reflects architectural responsibility rather than business importance.
