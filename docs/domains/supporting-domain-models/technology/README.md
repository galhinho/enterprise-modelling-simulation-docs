Status: 📝 Draft

---

# Purpose

The Technology Domain represents the resources, services, platforms, and systems used by an enterprise to enable and support its capabilities.

Technology exists to help an enterprise perform work, manage information, support participation, and deliver value.

The Technology Domain focuses on technology concepts rather than specific vendors, products, or implementations.

---

# Scope

The Technology Domain is responsible for understanding:

- Technology Resources
- Technology Services
- Technology Platforms
- Technology Dependencies
- Technology Ownership
- Technology Lifecycle

The domain focuses on the role technology plays within the enterprise and how technology supports business capabilities.

---

# Relationship to the Enterprise

Technology does not define business objectives, organizational structures, or enterprise capabilities.

Instead, Technology enables the implementation of enterprise capabilities.

Technology decisions may be influenced by:

- Business Objectives
- Business Capabilities
- Requirements
- Constraints
- Organizational Characteristics
- Governance
- Regulatory Considerations

Technology should therefore be viewed as an enabling domain within the enterprise.

---

# Architectural Observation

Technology is rarely implemented for its own sake.

Technology is typically introduced to support enterprise capabilities and operational needs.

As a result, technology selection and implementation decisions are often influenced by requirements, constraints, and organizational priorities rather than technology considerations alone.
