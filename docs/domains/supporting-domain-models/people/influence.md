# Influence

Status: 📝 Draft

---

## Overview

Influence represents the ability of an individual to affect decisions, behaviors, outcomes, relationships, and enterprise activities.

Influence may exist formally through authority or informally through expertise, trust, experience, relationships, or organizational dependence.

---

## Definition

Influence describes the capacity of a person to affect the actions, decisions, or outcomes of others within an enterprise context.

Influence is distinct from authority and should not be assumed to correlate directly with organizational hierarchy.

---

## Purpose

The purpose of Influence within the People Domain is to acknowledge that individuals may contribute differently to enterprise outcomes despite occupying similar positions or roles.

Influence helps explain why organizations with similar structures may behave differently.

---

## Characteristics

Influence:

- May be formal or informal.
- May change over time.
- Is not determined solely by authority.
- May emerge from expertise, trust, relationships, or experience.
- May affect enterprise resilience and dependency.
