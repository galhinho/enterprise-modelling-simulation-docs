# Experience

Status: 📝 Draft

---

## Overview

Experience represents practical exposure gained through participation in activities, responsibilities, events, decisions, and enterprise operations.

Experience contributes to judgment, familiarity, confidence, and effectiveness.

Experience may influence how individuals respond to situations and perform responsibilities.

---

## Definition

Experience represents accumulated practical exposure and lessons learned through participation and activity.

Experience reflects what an individual has encountered, performed, observed, or managed over time.

Experience is distinct from knowledge, although the two concepts are related.

---

## Purpose

The purpose of Experience within the People Domain is to represent the practical background that may influence an individual's effectiveness, decision-making, and readiness for responsibilities.

Experience helps explain why individuals with similar knowledge may perform differently in real-world situations.

---

## Characteristics

Experience:

- Is acquired through participation and activity.
- May be broad or specialized.
- May influence judgment and decision-making.
- May increase familiarity with responsibilities and environments.
- May contribute to confidence and effectiveness.
- May vary significantly between individuals.
- May become less relevant as environments change.

---

## Relationships

### People

Experience is accumulated by individuals throughout their participation in enterprise activities.

### Knowledge

Experience may contribute to the development, refinement, and application of knowledge.

### Influence

Experience may contribute to an individual's influence within the enterprise.

### Responsibilities

Certain responsibilities may require specific types or levels of experience.

### Organization

Organizations may depend upon experienced individuals for continuity, mentorship, and operational effectiveness.

### Knowledge

Experience may contribute to the development, refinement, and application of Knowledge.

---

## Design Principles

### Experience is Distinct from Knowledge

Experience and Knowledge are related but distinct concepts.

Experience represents practical exposure and lessons learned through participation and activity.

Knowledge represents information, understanding, expertise, and insight.

Experience may contribute to the development, refinement, and application of Knowledge, while Knowledge may influence how Experience is interpreted and applied.

Neither concept should be assumed to fully represent the other.

---

### Experience Develops Over Time

Experience should be viewed as an evolving characteristic rather than a static attribute.

---

### Experience May Influence Outcomes

Individuals with similar knowledge may produce different outcomes due to differences in experience.

---
