# People Relationships

Status: 📝 Draft

---

## Purpose

This document describes how the People Domain relates to other concepts and Domains within the Enterprise Modelling & Simulation.

People do not participate in isolation.

Their participation is shaped by the Enterprise, Business, Organization, Responsibilities, Identity, Knowledge, Technology, and relationships with other People.

---

## Enterprise Relationship

People participate within the Enterprise as human participants.

The Enterprise provides the broader context within which participation, assignments, responsibilities, identities, knowledge, and technology use occur.

Relationship:

Enterprise
    ↓
People

---

## Business Relationship

People perform activities and exercise judgment that contribute to Business Capabilities and Value delivery.

Business and Capability requirements may influence the number, types, skills, experience, and availability of People required by the Enterprise.

Relationship:

Business Capabilities
    ↓
People Requirements

---

## Organization Relationship

People participate in Organizational structures through assignments, positions, roles, teams, departments, and reporting relationships.

Organization defines structures and responsibility arrangements.

People occupy or participate through those structures without becoming identical to them.

Relationship:

Organization
    ↔
People

---

## Responsibility Relationship

People may receive Responsibilities directly or through Positions, Roles, Teams, and Assignments.

A Person requires sufficient Authority, Access, Resources, and organizational context to fulfil assigned Responsibilities.

Relationship:

Responsibility
    ↔
People

---

## Identity Relationship

Identity represents how a Person participates within the Enterprise.

A Person may hold one or more Identities, while an Identity may exist independently of a direct human participant in some enterprise contexts.

People and Identity remain distinct concepts.

Relationship:

People
    ↔
Identity

---

## Knowledge Relationship

People may acquire, create, hold, apply, share, and transfer Knowledge.

Knowledge does not belong to the People Domain.

Knowledge remains a standalone Supporting Domain that may also exist within documentation, technology, organizational artifacts, and enterprise memory.

Relationship:

People
    ↔
Knowledge

---

## Technology Relationship

People and Technology maintain a largely symbiotic relationship.

People design, select, operate, support, maintain, and evolve Technology.

Technology enables, augments, and influences how People perform work and participate within the Enterprise.

Relationship:

People
    ↔
Technology

---

## Company Relationship

A Company may establish a legal, contractual, commercial, or administrative relationship through which a Person participates.

Company is one possible Enterprise form and is not the universal context for People participation.

Relationship:

Company
    ↔
People

---

## People Relationships

People may maintain formal and informal relationships with other People.

Examples may include:

- Reporting relationships
- Collaboration
- Mentorship
- Trust
- Professional networks
- Informal leadership
- Dependency
- Conflict

These relationships may influence communication, Knowledge transfer, participation decisions, and enterprise outcomes.
