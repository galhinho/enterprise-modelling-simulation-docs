# People Domain

Status: 📝 Draft

---

## Purpose

The People Domain represents the human participants who contribute to, interact with, and are affected by the enterprise.

People perform work, exercise judgment, hold responsibilities, make decisions, use technology, and influence how the organization operates and evolves.

The People Domain provides the human perspective within the enterprise model.

---

## Scope

The People Domain focuses on:

- Human participants
- Roles and assignments
- Responsibilities
- Capabilities and skills
- Organizational participation
- Human relationships
- Lifecycle considerations
- Human influence within the enterprise

The domain intentionally distinguishes people from organizational structures, identities, and technologies while maintaining relationships with each.
