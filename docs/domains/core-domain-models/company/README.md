## Purpose

The Company model represents a commercial or legal organizational form that may exist within an Enterprise.

A Company may pursue Value objectives through Business, Business Capabilities, Organization, People, Identity, Knowledge, and Technology.

Company is not the universal root of the simulator and should not be assumed to represent every possible Enterprise.

Enterprises may also take other forms, including government agencies, non-profit organizations, educational institutions, healthcare providers, research organizations, and community organizations.

---

## Scope

The Company model focuses on:

- Commercial and legal identity
- Industry and sector
- Size and geographic presence
- Operating context
- Relationships with Enterprise Domains
- Company-specific lifecycle considerations

The Company model does not replace the responsibilities of Business, Organization, People, Identity, Knowledge, or Technology.
