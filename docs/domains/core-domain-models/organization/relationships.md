# Organization Relationships

Status: 📝 Draft

# Purpose

This document describes how the Organization Domain interacts with other domains within the Enterprise Modelling & Simulation.

Organizations do not exist independently and are responsible for coordinating people, resources, technology, and business activities.

---

# Relationship Overview

Organization serves as the operational structure through which business objectives are executed.

Many domains either influence or depend upon organizational structures.

---

# Knowledge Relationship

Organizations depend upon Knowledge to coordinate responsibilities, support decision-making, preserve organizational understanding, and deliver capabilities effectively.

Organizational structures may influence how Knowledge is created, distributed, retained, and accessed across teams, departments, roles, and responsibilities.

Knowledge may also describe the Organization itself through artifacts such as:

- Organizational charts
- Responsibility models
- Reporting relationships
- Governance documentation
- Policies
- Procedures
- Institutional memory

Relationship:

Organization
    ↔
Knowledge

The Organization Domain defines the structures and responsibilities through which Knowledge is coordinated.

The Knowledge Domain defines the information, understanding, expertise, and organizational memory used within those structures.

---

# Responsibilities

Responsibilities represent one of the foundational concepts of the Organization Domain.

Organizations allocate, coordinate, and govern responsibilities in support of business objectives.

Roles, teams, departments, authority structures, and accountability mechanisms emerge partly from the need to manage responsibilities effectively.
