Status: 📝 Draft

---

# Purpose

The Organization Domain defines how an enterprise structures and coordinates resources, responsibilities, and capabilities in pursuit of business objectives.

It focuses on the mechanisms that enable an enterprise to organize itself and operate effectively.

Within the Enterprise Modelling & Simulation, the Organization Domain represents the structures that support the delivery of business capabilities and value creation.

---

# Scope

This domain currently focuses on:

- Organizational Structure
- Responsibilities
- Accountability
- Authority
- Teams
- Departments
- Business Units
- Capability Coordination

Additional concepts may be introduced as the simulator evolves.

---

# Key Principle

Organizations exist to support business objectives and deliver business capabilities.

Organizations do not define what the enterprise must achieve.

Instead, they define how the enterprise structures itself to achieve those objectives.
